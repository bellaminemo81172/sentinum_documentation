The EOS Radar Series offers high-performance, wireless IoT level measurement based on precise radar technology. It enables reliable measurements of liquids, bulk solids, and piece goods up to 10 meters, even under demanding conditions.

Thanks to its flat design, the sensor is ideally suited for use on IBC tanks . The optional integrated 1.5-inch thread ensures quick and easy installation without additional adapters.

In addition to level measurement, the EOS Radar Series offers integrated localization functions via GPS. Combined with high-performance antenna technology, this enables exceptionally stable and long-range wireless communication.

The rugged housing is IP69K-rated, making it ideally suited for harsh industrial and outdoor applications. For maximum flexibility, the sensor features an injection-molded SMA connector as well as an optional M12 connector.

With support for mioty®, NB-IoT, LoRaWAN®, and LTE-CAT-M1, as well as flexibly configurable measurement and transmission intervals, the EOS Radar Series is the ideal solution for smart tank monitoring, industrial applications, and digital inventory monitoring.
