Compatible Level Probes

Easily integrate industrial 4–20 mA sensors

The EOS420 was specifically designed for connecting external hydrostatic level probes. Via the rugged M12 interface, standard industrial 4–20 mA sensors can be connected, powered, and monitored wirelessly using LoRaWAN®, mioty®, NB-IoT, or LTE-M connectivity.

  • Direct support for 4–20 mA level probes
  • 12 V sensor power supply provided by the EOS420
  • Compatible with leading manufacturers such as ifm

Features

Everything on board

Wireless standards

LoRaWAN, mioty & Cellular (NB-IoT / LTE-CAT-M1) — all in one device.

M12 connection

Rugged M12 connection for 4–20 mA level probes and external industrial sensors.

Auto Antenna Selector

Internal or external SMA antenna — automatic selection based on configuration.

IP69K

Dustproof and high-pressure waterproof. Overmolded SMA connector, vibration-resistant.

High-Performance GPS

Dedicated GNSS (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou) in all versions.

Accelerometer

Integrated sensor for shock detection and “Tracking on Activity.”

Power source

Replaceable 3×AA LiSOCl₂ or 2×C LiMnO₂ cells for extra-long runtime.

BLE Provisioning

Configuration and provisioning via Bluetooth or LoRaWAN downlink.

Flexible Intervals

Measurement and transmission intervals are freely configurable—ideal for long-term deployments.
