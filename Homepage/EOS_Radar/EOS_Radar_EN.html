Two Versions

EOS-R & EOS420

Non-contact radar or external 4–20 mA level probe—both versions share the same wireless technology, configuration, and cloud ecosystem.

60 GHz radar

EOS-R

Non-contact level measurement using pulse radar—directly on the surface, without contact with the medium.

  • Range up to 10 m · ±10 mm absolute, 1 mm resolution
  • Liquids & bulk materials · open tanks, shafts, bodies of water
  • Low-maintenance & wear-free · no contact with media
  • Dedicated GNSS in all radio variants
Applications: IBCs, tanks, diesel and oil tanks, fresh and wastewater, canals, rivers, chemical drums.
M12 · 4–20 mA

EOS420

Connection of external 4–20 mA level probes via M12 — for measurement points that the radar cannot reach.

  • For hydrostatic level probes · 12 V power supply, 4–20 mA
  • Wells, boreholes, and deep water · extra-long measurement depths
  • Tilt & Opening Detection · Motion-Based Tracking
  • Same wireless & cloud connectivity as EOS-R
Applications: Level measurements, groundwater monitoring stations, tank monitoring, construction sites, flood protection.

Two Algorithms · One Radar

Two measurement methods

Switchable via configuration — from clear liquid levels to diffuse bulk material surfaces.

Liquid Level

Amplitude over time
  • Amplitude analysis of the echo over time
  • Clear, well-defined surface reflections
  • Precise level measurements in tanks, shafts, and bodies of water

Smart Waste

Phase variance over time/distance
  • Jumps in phase variance over time and distance
  • Also detects objects with very faint echoes
  • Ideal for bulk materials, waste, and diffuse surfaces

Installation

Flexible Mounting Options

The right solution for every installation scenario—from M4 screw mounting to flange mounting. Matching mounting accessories available upon request.

M4 screw mounting

4× integrated M4 threaded inserts on the back. Permissible screw-in depth: 7.7 mm.

1.5″ process thread

“T” version with 1.5″ male thread — screws directly into existing adapters or sealing flanges.

Magnetic wall mounting

Neodymium magnets on a metal surface — tool-free, reversible, ideal for temporary applications.

Adhesive mounting

Double-sided industrial adhesive tape for smooth, dry surfaces — quick and residue-free.

Front-panel blind rivet nut

Preferred option: The sensor sits flush against the wall; battery and SIM card can be replaced without loosening any screws.

Flange mounting

For standard screw connections — max. 7.7 mm screw-in depth, ideal for tank flanges.

Wall mount

Optional stainless steel bracket (167.5 mm extension) for a defined distance from the measured material—also suitable for overhead mounting.

Alignment

Mount parallel to the measured object—non-horizontal alignment is compensated for via the level offset.
