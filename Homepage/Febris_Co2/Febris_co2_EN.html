Aerosol indicator

Air Quality & Virus Transmission

Aerosols in the air we breathe can transmit viruses and bacteria. Especially indoors, air quality is usually poorer than outdoors – increasing the risk of infection. CO₂ concentration correlates with aerosol levels and is therefore a reliable indicator.

  • Real-time monitoring: continuous CO₂ measurement in indoor air.
  • Warning system: automatic alerts when thresholds are exceeded – prompt to ventilate.
  • Data analysis: Evaluation via web app and API – identify patterns, optimize ventilation strategies.
