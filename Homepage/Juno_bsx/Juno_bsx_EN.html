    Use Cases

    Applications of the Juno BSX operating hours counter

    Monitoring of construction equipment

    ISO 14001ISO 50001Condition-based maintenanceReduced fuel consumption

    How it works

    For construction equipment, the Juno BSX records actual operating hours regardless of the location. The data is available digitally across all locations and enables transparent analysis of usage.

    Added Value & ISO Compliance

    Maintenance and service are performed strictly based on usage, not on a calendar schedule. This reduces fuel consumption, emissions, and wear and tear. Companies can deploy machines more efficiently, avoid downtime, and reliably document their energy-related metrics for ISO 50001, as well as reduce environmental impacts in accordance with ISO 14001.

    Monitoring of cranes and wire rope hoists

    Condition-based maintenanceTransparent operations managementOn/off times

    How it works

    The Juno BSX records the actual operating times of crane systems and automatically highlights the operating hours at which safety-related maintenance is required.

    Added Value & ISO Compliance

    Through runtime-based maintenance, safety-critical components are inspected in a targeted manner without unnecessary service calls. This increases operational safety, reduces component replacement, and supports a verifiable, efficient maintenance strategy.

    Monitoring of pump operating hours

    Condition-based maintenanceTransparent operations managementOperating and downtimeFail-safe

    How it works

    The Juno BSX not only records the operating hours of pumps but also reliably detects whether a pump is currently running or idle. The operating status is transmitted wirelessly and can be viewed centrally at any time. This makes it immediately clear whether a pump is active—for example, when water needs to be drained.

    Added Value & ISO Compliance

    The clear overview of runtime and operating status enables a rapid response to failures or downtime, e.g., during drainage or pumping operations. Maintenance and operation are performed as needed rather than on a calendar-based schedule. This reduces energy consumption, prevents damage caused by insufficient water drainage, and supports energy-efficient operations management.
