Orelia Features

Configure, alert, and analyze fill levels

Everything you need for reliable level monitoring—in a single web interface.

01 · Level Configuration

Create a tank once—understand it everywhere

Define the geometry, nominal volume, and density for each tank. Orelia automatically converts the raw measurement value into volume, weight, and percentage.

  • Container geometries: cylinders, cuboids, IBCs, silos
  • Conversion to volume (l/m³), weight (kg/t), and fill level in %
  • Density and offset calibration per asset
  • Grouping by location, route, or customer
02 · Warnings & Alarms

Early warning for filling and emptying

Define thresholds for each tank or group—Orelia automatically notifies you when refilling or emptying is required.

  • Custom thresholds for full, empty, and critical levels
  • Alarms via email, SMS, and webhook
  • Escalation levels and recipient lists
  • Trend detection: consumption per hour/day
03 · Reports & Documentation

Automated Documentation

Recurring reports, audit-ready exports, and a tamper-proof archive—for internal processes and external compliance requirements.

  • Automatic PDF reports (daily, weekly, monthly)
  • CSV export by time period, location, and container
  • Signed, tamper-proof archiving
  • Consumption and filling history per asset
04 · API

Data in Your ERP and Scheduling Systems

Do you already have an ERP, routing, or logistics system? Orelia delivers fill level data and alerts directly via REST or Webhook.

  • REST API for measurement values, assets, and reports
  • Webhook notifications for alarms and thresholds
  • Payload decoder available as an NPM package
Hardware

Two technologies, one IoT ecosystem

Choose the EOS variant that best suits your measurement task and installation location. Both sensors use the same platform, the same wireless standards, and the same cloud integrations.

60 GHz Radar

EOS-R

Non-contact level measurement using 60-GHz pulse radar—directly on the surface, without contact with the medium. For liquids and bulk materials, open containers, shafts, and bodies of water.

  • Range up to 10 m · ±10 mm absolute, 1 mm resolution
  • Liquids & bulk materials · IBCs, tanks, channels, silos
  • Low-maintenance & wear-free · no contact with media
  • IP69K · –25 °C to +85 °C · replaceable batteries
  • LoRaWAN®, mioty®, NB-IoT & LTE-M · Dedicated GNSS
Go to the EOS-R product page →
M12 · 4–20 mA

EOS420

Wireless connection of external hydrostatic level probes via the rugged M12 connector. Ideal for wells, boreholes, groundwater monitoring stations, tanks, and shafts—anywhere radar reaches its limits.

  • 4–20 mA input · M12 · Industry standard
  • 12 V sensor power supply for external probes (e.g., ifm PS3417)
  • IP69K · –20 °C to +80 °C · replaceable batteries
  • LoRaWAN®, mioty®, NB-IoT & LTE-M
  • Made in Germany
