Sentinum LuneX - Cloud Positioning Service for LoRaWAN Sensors

 What previously was time-consuming to decode, is now direct: Lunex converts NAV and Wi-Fi SSID scan data into valuable geospatial positions. With LuneX, we introduce a new cloud service specifically designed for energy-efficient IoT location tracking of LoRaWAN® devices based on the LR1110 series. The service decodes GNSS scan and WiFi data from LoRaWAN® devices and calculates precise geographic coordinates – entirely in the cloud. The result: reliable location data with minimal energy consumption.
