Technology

3D Laser, ML & Radar – Combined in the Apollon-Q

How we use 256 measurement points, intelligent algorithms, and radar technology to create the most accurate level measurement on the market.

3D laser measurement technology

Up to 256 measurement points produce a low-resolution 3D image – even non-uniform surfaces such as general cargo are captured with precision. The measurement window can be individually adjusted for each container.

Machine Learning

Intelligent algorithms and ML models combine the individual measurement values to determine a reliable fill level—even complex containers and difficult geometries are accurately mapped.

IoT Radar Sensor

The first size-optimized and cost-effective radar sensor for IoT applications. Measures through plastic walls, ideal for liquids, metals, and smooth surfaces.
