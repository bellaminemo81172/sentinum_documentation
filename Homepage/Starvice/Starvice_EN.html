What is Starvice?

A portal for your entire IoT world

Starvice does not display devices or data itself. It is a login, navigation, and shared framework—your gateway to specialized Sentinum IoT solutions.

Orelia · Tracking

Real-time outdoor asset tracking

Keep track of the location, movement, and status of your mobile assets—anywhere LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, or LTE-CAT-M1 is available.

  • Live map with geofencing and motion alerts
  • Self-powered trackers for multi-year use
  • Historical routes, heatmaps, and uptime analysis
  • Multi-connectivity: LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, LTE-CAT-M1

IOWA · IIoT Monitoring

Industrial IO-Link Monitoring Without Compromise

IOWA brings machine data, operating hours, and process values from production into a modern IIoT dashboard—vendor-independent and ready to use right away.

  • Directly connect and visualize IO-Link sensors
  • Vibration, pressure, flow, and temperature in real time
  • OEE analysis, maintenance windows, and threshold alarms
  • Interfaces to MES, ERP, and Datahub API

Envira · Environmental Monitoring

Precise visualization of environmental and temperature monitoring

Whether it’s a cold chain, warehouse, or remote station: Envira consolidates all relevant environmental data into a clear IoT solution—including weather, air quality, and precipitation.

  • Temperature Monitoring with a Complete History
  • Wind, precipitation, particulate matter, and CO₂ at a glance
  • Configurable thresholds and push notifications
  • Exportable reports for audits and compliance

Infrax · Smart Building

Centrally control building and indoor climate management

Infrax brings together sensors from throughout the building in a single IoT dashboard—from occupancy and indoor climate to energy consumption—wirelessly via LoRaWAN and mioty.

  • CO₂, temperature, humidity, and occupancy per room
  • Energy and consumption transparency for ISO 50001
  • Freely configurable zones, tenants, and floors
  • Wireless sensors via LoRaWAN and mioty
