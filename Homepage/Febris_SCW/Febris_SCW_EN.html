Dew Point & Wall Moisture

How mold prevention works

The Febris SCW combines room temperature, relative humidity, and wall surface temperature. From this, it calculates the dew point—that is, the point at which condensation forms on the wall and thus poses a risk of mold.

  • Example: 21 °C room, 60% RH → dew point ≈ 12.9 °C. At a wall temperature of 16 °C, this results in ≈ 82% RH on the wall – risk of mold.
  • Early warning: Before mold develops, the sensor issues a warning, allowing for timely action.
  • Automated ventilation schedules: Ventilation times are optimized based on data – better air quality, lower energy costs.
