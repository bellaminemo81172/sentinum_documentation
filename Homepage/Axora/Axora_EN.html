How Axora works

From sensor to automatic order

Four clear steps—no data gaps, no manual inventory, no major IT project.

01

Capture

Sensors, scales, RFID readers, and dispensing systems report inventory, consumption, and additional data to the Axora Cloud in real time.

02

Understand

The platform translates device logs into semantic business logic: containers, materials, location, consumption rate, and demand.

03

Decide

Configurable rules and demand forecasts identify replenishment, maintenance, and escalation needs before gaps arise.

04

Trigger

Alerts, orders, and ERP postings are triggered automatically—via webhook, API, or directly in the ERP system.
