About Sentinum

Sensor technology that matters.

Sentinum develops innovative sensor solutions based on connected sensors. Our story began with a clear vision: to make the world safer and smarter through modern sensor technologies. Founded by three visionary entrepreneurs, our company has quickly become a major player in the sensor industry.

We make sensor technology relevant by working closely with our customers. With a growing network of partners, we deliver relevant data and practical applications. Our goal is to make IoT projects easy to implement.

Through high-quality sensors and precise data, we increase the relevance and added value of smart services—across all industries.

The team behind Sentinum

Part of the ifm Group

Since our founding, we have been dedicated to the development and manufacture of innovative wireless sensors. Our team is constantly working to fully realize the potential of our technologies.

Since May 2024, Sentinum GmbH has been part of the renowned ifm Group —one of the world’s leading providers of industrial automation technology. This partnership strengthens our position as a leading provider in the sensor industry and enables us to implement even larger projects with the highest quality.
