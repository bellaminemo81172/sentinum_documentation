What is IOWA?

Industrial monitoring for IO-Link & 4–20 mA

IOWA connects to ThingsBoard and shows in real time what’s happening with your adapters and sensors—whether in a single facility, a factory, or spread across multiple locations.

Live device overview

All adapters & sensors at a glance – online/offline status, alarms, aliases, and product IDs in real time.

Sensor data & I/O mapping

Detailed IO-Link attributes, digital and analog signals per sensor – automatically decoded.

Historical trends

Graphical analysis of measured values over time – identify trends, analyze problems retrospectively.

Alarm overview

Central display of all active warnings – immediately identify where action is needed.

Plug & Play

Live in 2 steps

Connect the IO-Link sensor to Aion – data is automatically decoded and visualized in IOWA. No configuration, no PLC, no middleware.

Step 01

Aion + IO-Link Sensor

Connect an IO-Link or 4–20 mA sensor to the Aion adapter. IOWA automatically detects the device, loads the IODD, and decodes all attributes—pressure, temperature, distance, digital signals.
