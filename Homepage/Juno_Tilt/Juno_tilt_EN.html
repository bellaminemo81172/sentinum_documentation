Juno Tilt – Real-time flap opening & tilt

The Juno Tilt is a dedicated lid opening and tilt sensor—no GPS, no tracking hardware. It reacts instantly as soon as the lid is opened and transmits the event directly via LoRaWAN®, mioty®, or cellular networks. Rugged in an IP69k-rated housing.

Use Case · Flap Opening

When every second counts – instant alarm upon opening

Shafts, distribution cabinets, containers, shipping boxes, or construction equipment – the Juno Tilt detects openings and changes in tilt in real time and immediately sends an event via LoRaWAN®, mioty®, or cellular networks.

  • Instant event upon opening – no polling delay
  • Detects permanently open flaps and counts openings
  • Vandalism and tamper detection via tilt sensor
  • Alarms via email, SMS, or webhook to existing systems
