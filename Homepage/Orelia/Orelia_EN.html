What is ORELIA?

A platform for all IoT assets

From trackers to level sensors—ORELIA aggregates, visualizes, and controls your devices from a single web interface.

Use Case 01

Real-time asset tracking

Where is my container, my tracker, my machine? ORELIA visualizes the position, movement, and history of every asset—using a combination of GNSS, cell-based location, and Wi-Fi tracking.

  • 3 location methods: GNSS (< 5 m), Cell-Locate, WiFi (15–40 m).
  • Route playback: Play back historical movement history on the map.
  • Geofencing: Up to 10 zones per device, alert upon entry/exit.
  • Accuracy circles: Visualization of the error radius depending on the method.
Discover compatible trackers
Use Case 02

Fully automatic level monitoring

Silos, tanks, containers, liquids, or bulk materials—ORELIA automatically calculates liters and percentages based on raw distance. With route planning, alerts, and multiple measurement principles.

  • Measurement principles: ToF (laser), radar, ultrasound – depending on the sensor.
  • Geometries: Cylinder, cuboid, sphere – volume calculated automatically.
  • Bulk calibration: Set empty and full distances for entire groups.
  • Alarm classes 0–3: critical, low, normal, full – via email/SMS.
