What is IO-Link?

The open standard for automation

IO-Link is a standardized communication protocol for automation technology. It connects control systems bidirectionally with intelligent field devices such as sensors and actuators—via a simple 3-wire connection.

Simple wiring
Standard 3-wire cables instead of expensive special cabling.
Plug & Play
Devices are integrated into the network without additional configuration.
Diagnostics & Parameters
Detailed remote diagnostics and parameter access via the controller.
Also for "dumb" devices
Conventional sensors can be integrated via an IO-Link master.
Why mioty®?

mioty® – Next-generation LPWAN

mioty® uses telegram splitting: Data packets are broken down into many sub-packets and transmitted independently over different frequencies. This makes the transmission extremely robust against interference—even in dense radio environments.

Long range

Up to 15 km in cities, over 100 km in rural areas.

Scalability

Millions of devices per network cell – without any loss of performance.

Low energy consumption

Several years of runtime on a single battery.

Robust & reliable

Telegram splitting & DSSS – stable even with many sources of interference.

Security

End-to-end encryption and device authentication.

Cost-effective

Simple, low-cost devices – ideal for large-scale IoT rollouts.
