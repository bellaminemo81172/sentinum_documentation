    4. Installation and Preparation

    Note

    If the sensor remains easily accessible even after installation, install the sensor first and activate it only after installation.

    If the sensor is no longer accessible after installation, activate the sensor first and then install it afterward.

    1. If you have ordered a device with an external antenna, which can be identified by the gold-colored RP-SMA connector, first install the antenna included in the package.
    2. The sensor can be mounted either horizontally or vertically . For wall or magnetic mounting, use the mounting holes provided in the side tabs of the housing.
    3. Depending on the sensor model and variant, the antenna and sensor connections may be located on the long or short side of the housing.
    Note

    External antenna: Please note that the antenna must always must be mounted vertically and its tip must point toward the ceiling. Tilt the antenna at the joint so that it is always aligned vertically. The external antenna is usually located on the long side of the housing. The antenna should maintain a minimum distance of 2 cm from metal surfaces. Make sure that the antenna is not shielded by surrounding metal parts, provided that the application allows it.

    Internal Antenna: If your device has an internal antenna (no external antenna visible), the Helios sensor must always be mounted with the long side oriented vertically, as this ensures maximum signal strength. The antenna is located on the underside (opposite the logo side) of the housing and should also be kept at least 2 cm away from metal surfaces. Make sure the antenna is not shielded by surrounding metal parts, as long as the application permits.

    Important

    If you have a device with an external antenna, the device must never be operated without the antenna connected. Otherwise, the sensor may be irreparably damaged.

    7. NFC Setup and Location of the NFC Tag

    Note

    You can use the LinQs NFC app to configure parameters . Please make sure you know where the NFC reader on your mobile device is located. Depending on the manufacturer and model, it may be located at the top, middle, or bottom of the device’s back. To locate the NFC tag, select “Read” in the app, for example, and slowly move the smartphone along the sensor.

    1. Activation is performed via an NFC app. This requires a smartphone. The app can be downloaded from the respective app stores . Simply search for Sentinum LinQs and download the app.
    2. First, locate the NFC tag on the sensor, and then locate the NFC reader on your device. The position of the NFC tag is shown in the following figure.
    3. Open the app and activate the sensor. To start the sensor with the default settings, select the Activate Sensor button in the app’s start menu. Then place your device on the sensor’s NFC tag.

      If the sensor is successfully activated, the message Sensor Updated! appears. Afterward, additional sensors can be activated.

    8. Setup Without a Mobile Device

    Note

    If you do not have a smartphone on hand, you can restart the sensor directly on the device. To do this, slide the marked switch to the Off position, wait at least 10 seconds, and then slide it back to the On position. The sensor will then restart and resume normal operation, such as the LoRaWAN® join procedure.

    9. Battery Replacement

    1. Open the housing with a suitable Phillips screwdriver. Carefully remove the cover and be careful not to damage the gasket.
    2. Before replacing the battery, turn off the sensor by flipping the switch to the Off position.

      Then carefully remove the batteries from the designated holder and insert new batteries.

      To achieve the specified runtime and performance, the following primary cells are approved:

      • Energizer® Ultimate Lithium AA
      • VARTA ULTRA LITHIUM Mignon AA
    3. Dispose of the old batteries properly after replacement . Then close the housing again and make sure the seal is correctly seated in its original position.
    Caution

    If battery types other than those recommended are used, performance, runtime, and product safety may be compromised .
