EOS420 General Description

The EOS420 series offers high-performance, wireless IoT level measurement based on external 4–20 mA sensors, such as hydrostatic level probes. It enables reliable measurements of liquids, even under demanding conditions, and is ideal for continuous level monitoring in tanks, containers, and shafts.

The robust M12 connector allows for the simple and secure integration of a wide variety of industrial 4–20 mA sensors.

Thanks to its flat design, the system is ideally suited for use on IBC tanks. The optional integrated 1.5-inch thread enables quick and easy installation without additional adapters.

In addition to level detection, the EOS420 series offers integrated location tracking via GPS. In combination with high-performance antenna technology, it enables exceptionally stable and long-range wireless communication.

The rugged housing is IP69K-rated and thus optimally designed for harsh industrial and outdoor applications. For maximum flexibility, the device features an in-mold SMA connector as well as an M12 connector for connecting external sensors.

With support for mioty®, NB-IoT, LoRaWAN®, and LTE-CAT-M1, as well as flexibly configurable measurement and transmission intervals, the EOS Series is the ideal solution for smart tank monitoring, industrial applications, and digital inventory monitoring.
