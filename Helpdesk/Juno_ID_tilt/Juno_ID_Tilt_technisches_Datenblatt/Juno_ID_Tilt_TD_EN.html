2. Overview

The Juno IP69k ID/TILT is designed for reliable tilt detection and opening detection of flaps and doors. The sensor does not have its own tracking mechanisms and must be tracked passively via the respective network. The Juno ID/TILT is available with either mioty® or LoRaWAN®. With a precise tilt sensor, it can reliably detect flap openings . Thanks to its robust design and IP69k protection rating, it is ideal for use in demanding environments and withstands dust, water, and high pressure.
