2. Features

  • mioty® or LoRaWAN® wireless interface (Modbus or Wireless M-Bus upon request)
  • Bidirectional meter (supply and consumption)
  • MID B + D certification for billing purposes
  • Environmental conditions Mechanical: M2
  • 1 A and 5 A current transformer connections for up to 20,000/5 A or 4,000/1 A. The transformation ratio can be configured multiple times via sealable buttons.
  • Direct connection up to 100 A
  • 2- or 4-rate operation (configurable on the meter)
  • High-current Opto Power MOSFET
  • S0 pulse output, 5 to 60 V AC/DC
  • Backlit graphical LCD (38 × 28 mm)
  • Dynamic 8-digit display with up to three decimal places

3. MID approval for billing purposes

The Hyperion has been tested in accordance with MID Modules B + D (Measurement Instruments Directive 2004/22/EC of the European Commission) and approved for billing purposes.

4. Display Operation

A backlit graphical LCD display measuring 38 × 28 mm allows for easy reading of measurement values and settings even under unfavorable lighting conditions.

The desired menu language can be selected using the control buttons . The clear and intuitive user interface simplifies both the initial setup and the daily operation of the energy meter.

6. Features

  • Logbook for events relevant to metrology regulations and configuration changes
  • Change in time or date
  • Change in current transformer ratio
  • Change to pulse rate and pulse duration
  • Change to voltage transformer ratio
  • Buffered internal real-time clock

The internal clock is maintained by a buffer in the event of a power failure. The load profile is saved every 15 minutes . The saved data can be read out via the available interfaces or viewed directly on the display.
